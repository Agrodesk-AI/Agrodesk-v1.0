# Interview guides

## Dealers (5)
1. Walk me through what happens when a new lead arrives. Who calls, when, how is it recorded?
2. What happens to leads you never call back? Why?
3. How do you track dues, service and used stock today?
4. Which ad/lead sources work? How do you know?
5. What do you use today (CRM, WhatsApp, register)? What do you pay?
6. Would you let an AI assistant message your customers? What worries you?
7. What would you pay monthly for fewer forgotten leads?
8. OEM restrictions on tools or customer contact?

## Farmers (5-10)
1. How did you find your last tractor? Who did you trust?
2. What do you want to know before buying (price, subsidy, used, service)?
3. Do you use WhatsApp, voice notes? Marathi or Hindi?
4. Would you ask an AI assistant on WhatsApp? What would make you trust it?
5. Have dealers' calls or messages annoyed you? When?
6. How do you feel about sharing your number so a dealer can contact you?
